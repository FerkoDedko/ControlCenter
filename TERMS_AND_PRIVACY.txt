1. WHAT THIS IS
Command Center is a free tool by [FELIXKRAL] that shows how your PC is doing and lets you
control it with your voice. It is not made by, or connected to, Microsoft, Google, Spotify
or Discord. Those names belong to their owners.

2. USE AT YOUR OWN RISK
The software is provided "as is", without any warranty. Some features change things on your
PC: Cleanup deletes the files you tick, End task closes programs (unsaved work is lost),
and Startup Manager changes what starts with Windows. The author is not responsible for
lost data or other damage. Read what a button does before you press it.

3. WHAT STAYS ON YOUR PC
Your settings, launcher items and voice commands are stored in
%APPDATA%\CommandCenter. Command Center has no account, no analytics and no tracking, and it
does not upload your files, process list or hardware information anywhere.

4. WHAT LEAVES YOUR PC (only when you switch the feature on)
- Voice commands: while voice recognition is switched on, short recordings of what you say
  are sent to Google's free speech-recognition service to be turned into text. Google's own
  privacy policy applies to that audio. Switch "Allow voice recognition" off and nothing is
  recorded or sent. The microphone is only used while Voice Control is listening.
- Weather: a request to wttr.in. Like any website, it sees your IP address and uses it to
  guess your city.
- News: a request to the BBC News headlines feed (feeds.bbci.co.uk).
- Opening links: Spotify, websites and other apps you launch work as they normally do.

5. DISCORD
Command Center reads the text of Discord pop-up notifications that Windows shows you, on
your PC only. Nothing is sent to anyone. To reply, it types into your own Discord app, as
if you did it yourself. It never sees your Discord password or token.
Discord's own rules may restrict automated messages (for example automatic busy replies).
You are responsible for following the rules of the services you use. Do not use it to spam.
